# Homepage image provenance

The photographs in this directory were extracted from the user-supplied Theos website flow PDF. Gallery images retain their original marks and are visual references, not verified Theos client projects. Four depth maps were prepared with Magnific for the opening scenes. The architectural image supplies both states of the blueprint reveal; its depth map (`architecture-depth.png`) was prepared with Magnific (Depth Anything 3) for the parallax. `Interiorimg.png` is the as-built interior generated with ChatGPT from the study as reference (a visual study, not a completed project); `Interiorimg-depth.png` is its Magnific depth map. The spiral reference is a fallback; the live spiral is procedural Three.js geometry.

Original reference documents remain in the local cleanup recovery archive. Detailed mapping is recorded in the redesign documents under `docs/`. `eye-portal.jpg` is the eye world with its carved wall opened into an arch onto the bedroom world, generated with ChatGPT from `eye.jpg` and `bedroom.jpg` as references (the source export is `imagegen.png`); `eye-portal-depth.png` is its Magnific depth map. `architecture-plan.jpg` is the blueprint state of the architecture section, generated with ChatGPT from `Interiorimg.png` so the two are pixel-aligned (source export `architecture-plan.png`).


`rhino.png` is the opening world (a bright modern living room, supplied by the user; the file keeps its old name); `rhino.jpg` is its 1920×1080 compressed copy used by the site, and `rhino-depth.png` was made locally with Depth Anything V2 (base). The previous opening image and its depth map are kept in `docs/snapshots/hero-before/`.

`section1-img.png` is the current opening world (a navy and ivory dining bar) and `section3-img.png` the third world (a white colonnaded salon), both supplied by the user. `section1.jpg` and `section3.jpg` are their compressed copies used by the site; `section1-depth.png` and `section3-depth.png` are Magnific depth maps (Depth Anything 3, near is dark), reduced to 768px. They replace `rhino2` and `bedroom` (the bedroom files remain here but are no longer used).
