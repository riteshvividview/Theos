Mobile hero images

On a phone the hero is tall and narrow (about 9:19), so the wide desktop images lose most of their width. These portrait versions keep the subject whole. For each one, attach the desktop image named below as the reference, then make its depth map from the new portrait image.

  • Size: 1080 × 1920 (9:16), portrait. The site trims a little from the top and bottom on taller phones, so keep the subject in the middle 80%.
  • Leave the area named in each prompt plain and light: the headline sits there.
  • Depth maps: same size as their image, near = dark, far = light (the site reads them this way round).
  • Save as the file names given, in public/images/redesign/, and tell me.

1. Home: the elephant hall

Image prompt

Recreate the attached interior as a tall portrait image, exactly 1080 × 1920 pixels, keeping the same place, materials, light and style: a white carved-stone hall with a monumental elephant head sculpted into the rock wall, thin waterfalls falling from the rock into a turquoise indoor pool, hanging vines, white sofas with navy cushions, blue-leaved plants in white planters, and a floor-to-ceiling glass wall onto a sunlit forest. Re-compose it vertically: the elephant head and its tusks centred in the middle third of the frame, the pool and one lounge chair with a lantern in the lower third, and the upper third mostly the bright glass wall and soft forest light, plain and airy so a headline can sit there. Photorealistic architectural render, soft daylight, high detail, no people, no text, no logos.
